# Skill: new-client-onboarding

## Trigger

Invoked when a rep signs a new fractional engagement and needs a structured ramp plan before their first week begins.

---

## Inputs Required

Four inputs. Ask for any that are missing.

1. **What they sell** — the client's product or service, 1–3 sentences
2. **Their target market** — who they sell to (industry, company size, buyer title)
3. **Their CRM** — which system they use (HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, spreadsheet, none, etc.)
4. **Agreed scope of work** — what the rep is responsible for (outbound only, full-cycle, SDR management, pipeline + close, etc.)

---

## Output

Three sections. A structured 30-day ramp, a week-one asset request list, and five foundational questions the rep must answer before making a prospecting call.

---

## Writing Rules

**Banned words and phrases:**
leverage, synergies, robust, cutting-edge, delve, seamless, streamline, empower, hit the ground running, onboarding journey, deep dive, circle back, touch base, move the needle, value-add, align, alignment, best practices, world-class.

**Specificity rule:** Every milestone in the ramp plan must describe a specific output — not an activity. "Complete discovery of target accounts" is an activity. "Identify 50 target accounts matching ICP criteria and loaded into CRM with contact owner assigned" is a milestone. If the input doesn't contain enough detail to be specific, note what needs to be confirmed with the client before the milestone can be set.

**CRM-aware:** If the rep provided a specific CRM, all references to pipeline and tracking use that system's terminology. If "none" or "spreadsheet," suggest a lightweight structure without prescribing a tool.

---

## Section 1: 30-Day Ramp Plan

Four weeks. Each week has 2–4 milestones. Milestones are outputs, not tasks.

Week 1 is always infrastructure — accounts, assets, access, clarity.
Week 2 is activation — first outreach, first calls, first feedback loop.
Week 3 is iteration — what's working, what's not, adjust.
Week 4 is operating rhythm — the rep should be in full motion by end of day 30.

Format each week as:

**Week [n] — [Theme]**
- [Milestone 1 — specific output]
- [Milestone 2]
- [Milestone 3 if applicable]

---

## Section 2: Week One Asset Request List

What the rep needs from the client before prospecting begins. Formatted as a list the rep can paste into an email or Slack message to the client.

Include only what is genuinely necessary — do not pad. If the rep is doing outbound only, they don't need the full sales playbook. If they're doing full-cycle, they need more.

Categories to consider (include only what applies to this scope):
- ICP documentation or past customer list
- Messaging, decks, or case studies
- CRM access and field definitions
- Competitive landscape notes
- Pricing and proposal templates
- Introduction to internal stakeholders (who should the rep meet in week one?)
- Any existing sequences or templates in use

Format as a numbered list, ready to copy-paste:

**Week One — Assets Needed from You**
1. [Asset — one line on why it's needed and how the rep will use it]
2. ...

---

## Section 3: Five Questions to Answer Before Prospecting

Five questions the rep must be able to answer clearly before making a single call or sending a single email. These are not research tasks — they are clarity tests. If the rep can't answer them after week one, they are not ready to prospect.

The questions are derived from the client's specific inputs — not a generic checklist. They should reflect the actual gaps or ambiguities in the information provided.

Format each as:

**Q[n]:** [Question]
*Why it matters: [One sentence on what breaks if this question isn't answered.]*

---

## Quality Check (Run Before Delivering)

- [ ] All ramp milestones describe outputs, not activities
- [ ] Week one is focused on infrastructure, not prospecting
- [ ] Asset request list maps to the agreed scope of work — nothing extraneous
- [ ] Five questions are specific to this client's inputs, not a generic checklist
- [ ] No banned words
- [ ] CRM terminology matches the system provided

---
