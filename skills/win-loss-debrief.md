# Skill: win-loss-debrief

## Trigger

Invoked after a deal closes — won or lost — and the rep needs to extract what actually happened and what to carry forward.

---

## Inputs Required

Five inputs. Accept partial input and work with what's provided — note any gaps.

1. **Outcome** — won or lost
2. **Deal size** — dollar amount or range
3. **Time in pipeline** — how long from first contact to close (or to loss)
4. **Key stakeholders** — names and roles of everyone involved on the prospect's side
5. **Rep's notes** — what happened, in the rep's own words — as detailed or as brief as they can provide

---

## Output

A structured debrief with four sections. This is a working document for the rep — not a post-mortem for a manager. The goal is to extract one actionable thing from every deal outcome and feed it back into the rep's process.

---

## Writing Rules

**Banned words and phrases:**
leverage, synergies, robust, cutting-edge, delve, seamless, streamline, empower, game-changer, lessons learned, going forward, moving forward, at the end of the day, key takeaways, actionable insights, it is what it is, these things happen, tough loss, great win, everything happens for a reason, we gave it our best.

**Accuracy rule:** The debrief must be grounded in the rep's notes. Do not invent explanations for outcomes. Where the notes don't support a conclusion, say so — "Not enough information to determine" is a valid output. Do not fill gaps with generic sales psychology.

**Tone:** Honest, analytical, neutral. Not self-congratulatory on wins. Not self-flagellating on losses. The rep is a professional reviewing tape.

**The real driver rule:** The first section — "What Actually Drove the Outcome" — must name one primary cause. Not a list of contributing factors. One cause. The discipline of picking one forces clarity. If it's genuinely impossible to isolate one cause, name the two most significant and explain why they're inseparable.

---

## Section Logic

### What Actually Drove the Outcome
Not what the rep did right or wrong — what actually determined whether this deal closed. This could be: a stakeholder dynamic, a timing factor, a competitive situation, a pricing issue, a process failure, a champion who did or didn't show up. Name it specifically and trace it back to a moment or decision in the pipeline.

### What the Rep Would Do Differently
One or two specific changes to make next time. Not "build more rapport" — name the moment where a different action would have changed the trajectory.

For wins: what would have gotten here faster or at a higher price?
For losses: what was the decision point the rep missed or misread?

### One Pattern to Replicate or Avoid
The single most transferable insight from this deal. Something the rep can apply to the next five deals. Specific enough to be actionable.

- **Wins:** Pattern to replicate — the thing that made this one close
- **Losses:** Pattern to avoid — the thing that killed it

### ICP Update — One Line
Based on this outcome, one sentence to add or modify in the rep's ICP. This could be:
- A company characteristic that predicted fit or misfit
- A stakeholder pattern that predicted a close or a stall
- A timing signal that was present or absent
- A disqualifying factor that wasn't in the ICP before

Format: "Add to ICP: [specific characteristic] [predicts / is a red flag for] [outcome]."

---

## Quality Check (Run Before Delivering)

- [ ] "What Actually Drove the Outcome" names one primary cause, not a list
- [ ] "What the Rep Would Do Differently" names specific moments, not general behaviors
- [ ] Pattern is transferable — could be applied to the next deal starting tomorrow
- [ ] ICP update is one line, specific, and in ICP-entry format
- [ ] No banned words
- [ ] No gap-filling — "Not enough information to determine" used where warranted
- [ ] Tone is analytical, not emotional

---

## Output Format

Deliver with no preamble. Use this exact structure:

---

### Win/Loss Debrief — [Company Name] | [Won / Lost] | [Deal Size] | [Time in Pipeline]
**Stakeholders:** [names and roles]
**Date:** [today's date]

---

**What Actually Drove the Outcome**
[One primary cause — specific, traced to a moment or decision. 3–6 sentences.]

---

**What the Rep Would Do Differently**
[1–2 specific changes — named to a moment in the pipeline, not a general behavior. 3–5 sentences.]

---

**One Pattern to [Replicate / Avoid]**
[The single most transferable insight. 2–3 sentences. Specific enough to apply tomorrow.]

---

**ICP Update**
Add to ICP: [one line in ICP-entry format]
