# Skill: linkedin-profile-optimizer

## Trigger

Invoked when a fractional sales rep wants to update their LinkedIn profile, reposition for fractional work, or refresh copy that reads as full-time-employment-focused.

---

## Inputs Required

Three inputs. Ask for any that are missing.

1. **Current LinkedIn headline** — paste the exact current text
2. **Current About section** — paste the full current text
3. **Current role description** — the most recent position description as it appears on LinkedIn

Optional but useful: what types of clients the rep is targeting, what outcomes they deliver, and any notable wins or client names they can reference.

---

## Output

Three rewritten sections. Ready to paste directly into LinkedIn. No commentary, no explanation of choices — just the copy.

---

## Positioning Principles

This profile is for a **fractional sales operator** — someone who runs revenue functions for multiple clients simultaneously, not a full-time employee looking for a job. Every word must reflect this.

**What fractional profiles get wrong (avoid all of these):**
- Reads like a resume summary ("Results-driven sales leader with 15 years of experience...")
- Vague claims without proof ("Proven track record of exceeding targets")
- FTE-seeking signals ("Open to new opportunities," "Available for full-time roles")
- Feature-listing instead of outcome-stating ("Expert in HubSpot, Salesforce, outbound sequencing...")
- Generic industry language that could describe anyone in sales

**What a strong fractional profile does:**
- States specifically who you work with and what you deliver
- Signals that you operate across multiple engagements, not one employer
- Leads with outcomes (revenue built, ramp time reduced, pipeline created) not activities
- Reads like a business operator, not a job candidate
- Makes the right client think "this person can step in and do this for us"

---

## Writing Rules

**Banned words and phrases:**
leverage, synergies, robust, cutting-edge, delve, seamless, streamline, empower, game-changer, transformative, results-driven, proven track record, passionate about, excited to, thought leader, guru, ninja, rockstar, dynamic, strategic thinker, detail-oriented, team player, hardworking, go-getter, self-starter, exceed expectations, move the needle, value-add, best-in-class, world-class, visionary.

**Tone:** Credible, specific, operating as a business. Reads like someone who has done the work and knows what it produces — not someone selling themselves.

**Numbers rule:** Where the rep provided specific results (revenue, percentages, deal counts, client names), use them. Where they didn't, write in language that implies specificity without fabricating numbers ("built pipeline from zero to qualified" not "grew pipeline by 300%").

---

## Output 1: Headline

**Constraints:**
- Under 120 characters (LinkedIn hard limit)
- No job title format ("VP of Sales | Open to Work")
- No generic descriptors ("Sales Expert | Revenue Growth | B2B")
- Must communicate: who you work with + what you do for them, or the outcome you deliver
- Written for the client reading it, not the recruiter

**Structure options** (use whichever fits the rep's actual work):
- [What you do] for [who] → [outcome]
- Fractional [function] for [client type] — [specific result or focus]
- [Specific outcome] for [company type] — [how you deliver it]

---

## Output 2: About Section

**Constraints:**
- Under 300 words
- First line must be a hook — the first 2–3 lines show before "see more," so they must earn the click
- No third-person narration
- No career history recap — that's what the experience section is for
- Structure: hook → what you do and who you do it for → how you work (fractional model) → proof or outcomes → one-line close with a clear action

---

## Output 3: Current Role Description

**Constraints:**
- Under 150 words
- Positioned as the active business, not a job
- Leads with what the role produces for clients, not a list of responsibilities
- 2–4 bullet points of specific outcomes or capabilities, followed by 1–2 sentences on the engagement model
- No "Responsible for..." or "Duties included..."

---

## Quality Check (Run Before Delivering)

- [ ] Headline is under 120 characters
- [ ] About section is under 300 words
- [ ] Role description is under 150 words
- [ ] No banned words in any section
- [ ] No FTE-seeking signals anywhere
- [ ] Headline communicates who the rep works with and what they deliver
- [ ] About section hook earns the "see more" click in first 2 lines
- [ ] All claims are either sourced from the rep's input or written without fabricated numbers
- [ ] Profile reads as an operating business, not a job application
