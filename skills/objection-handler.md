# Skill: objection-handler

## Trigger

Invoked when a rep receives a specific objection during a call or in writing and needs a set of calibrated responses to choose from.

---

## Input Required

The objection, word for word as the prospect said or wrote it. The more exact the language, the better the responses.

Optional but useful:
- Where in the conversation it came up (opener, mid-discovery, proposal stage, close)
- What the rep's client sells (if not established earlier in session)
- Any context about the prospect's situation

---

## Output

Three responses at different pressure levels. Each under 50 words. Each leads with a question or an acknowledgment that invites the prospect to say more — not a statement that defends or deflects.

---

## Diagnostic Questioning Influence

Diagnostic Questioning responses do not fight objections. They redirect the prospect's thinking by asking questions that surface what the objection is actually about. Most objections are surface expressions of a deeper uncertainty — price objections are often risk objections, timing objections are often priority objections. The responses should create space for the real issue to emerge.

---

## Pressure Level Definitions

**Soft — Acknowledge and Redirect**
The lowest friction response. Validates what the prospect said without argument, then asks a question that reopens the conversation. Used when the relationship needs preserving or the objection came early and might not be real yet.

**Medium — Reframe with Evidence**
Accepts the surface objection and offers a reframe — a different way to look at it — backed by a specific fact, outcome, or observation. Ends with a question. Used when the prospect is genuinely uncertain and open to new information.

**Direct — Name the Real Issue**
Calls out what the objection is likely actually about. Not aggressive — honest. Used when the rep has enough rapport to be direct, or when softer approaches haven't worked. Still ends with a question, not a close.

---

## Writing Rules

**Banned words and phrases:**
leverage, synergies, robust, cutting-edge, delve, seamless, I understand your concern, that's a great point, I hear you (empty), a lot of our clients felt the same way, what if I told you, the thing is, at the end of the day, circle back, touch base, just to clarify, with all due respect, pain points (name the specific issue).

**Tone:** Calm, unhurried, undefensive. The rep is not rattled by this objection. They've heard it before and they're genuinely curious about what's behind it.

**Under 50 words each:** Count before delivering. If a response exceeds 50 words, cut the least specific sentence.

**Question rule:** Every response ends with a question. The question does not pitch. It opens, clarifies, or redirects.

---

## Quality Check (Run Before Delivering)

- [ ] Each response is under 50 words
- [ ] Each response leads with an acknowledgment or a question, not a defense
- [ ] Each response ends with a question
- [ ] No banned words in any response
- [ ] Three responses are meaningfully different in tone and approach — not the same response with different wording
- [ ] The direct response names the likely real issue, not the surface objection

---
